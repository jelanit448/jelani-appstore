Privacy Policy (version 2026-10-11.v1)

What we collect

Account details you provide (username, email, birthdate for the 13+ check), apps you upload, purchases, reviews, and standard server logs. Passwords are stored as bcrypt hashes — never in plain text.

What we don't do

No ads, no trackers, no sale of your data. KYC identity notes are used only for payout verification.

Money

Until Stripe Connect is configured, balances are demo ledger records stored on this server. They are not bank deposits and cannot be withdrawn.

Your rights

Ask the operator to export or delete your account data at any time. Deletion removes your profile; ledger rows are kept in anonymized form for accounting integrity.

Children

The store is 13+. Accounts found to belong to under-13s are removed.
